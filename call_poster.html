Call for Posters

Important Dates

Poster abstracts due: October, 1, 2025, (Anywhere on Earth)

Notification of acceptance: October, 7, 2025

Overview

Posters at NPC '24 will be selected on the basis of submitted abstracts. Posters will be selected in advance of the conference. An abstract should be a one-page PDF (excluding references). You may use the template Springer-LNCS template. Abstracts must be submitted by October, 1, 2025,via the EasyChair: https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=npc2025. Submissions should be aligned with the topics of interest to NPC. Submissions should represent early work that has not yet been published in a refereed conference or journal. Do not email the abstract.

Authors will be notified of acceptance or rejection via email by October, 7, 2025. No reviews will be provided. Send any questions about the poster session to ifipnpc2025@gmail.com.
